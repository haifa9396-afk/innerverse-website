FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Email myinnerverse.world@gmail.com.

Is Innerverse free?

Yes, you can start for free with The Beginning. Premium unlocks the deeper levels and is available as a monthly subscription or a one-time purchase.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet. Innerverse is on Android today and the iPhone version is coming. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the day it's out.

Is this a psychological test or therapy?

No. Innerverse is a self-reflection tool. It isn't a clinical assessment and isn't a replacement for a therapist or medical care.

What does the reflection guide do with my writing?

It uses your profile and what you write in a session to respond to you. Your sessions are saved to your account so you can come back to them.

Can I use it on more than one phone?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and your progress is restored.
